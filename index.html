Bard • Storyteller • Wanderer

Vanir

A charismatic bard who believes a melody can open doors no blade ever could — and that the right song can turn the tide of a battle.

Music MagicSupportFree Spirit
Read Vanir's story +

Vanir grew up among traveling performers, learning early that stories and songs could reach people in ways force never could. He now wanders in search of forgotten tales, beautiful melodies and people worth inspiring, carrying a dark lute and a restless love of freedom.

When a performance truly matters, Vanir closes his eyes and gives himself completely to the music. In those moments, melody becomes magic: his presence swells through the battlefield, strengthening his companions and turning courage into something almost tangible. Behind the easy smile is a heart carrying more stories than he could ever tell in one lifetime.

Arcane Warden • Veteran • Mage Hunter

Eldric Vane

Once a captain who hunted forbidden magic, Eldric became bound to the very arcane power he had spent years trying to destroy.

The Ashen HandArcane FlameMachinery
Read Eldric's story +

Once known as Captain Eldric Vane, he served as a military engineer and mage hunter during the war between industrial kingdoms and forbidden arcane cults. Convinced that magic was too dangerous to remain unchecked, he hunted sorcerers, burned forbidden libraries and destroyed ancient relics with enchanted firearms and experimental machinery.

Deep beneath the ruins of Hollowreach, Eldric discovered the Ashen Heart — a living arcane flame older than any known kingdom. When he tried to destroy it, the artifact erupted. The blast took his right arm and scarred part of his soul, but the flame did not kill him. It chose to stay. Now the former hunter carries the impossible power he once feared, earning the name The Ashen Hand.
